# Best.Life — phase-wise build prompt

Use these prompts in order. Each phase ends with a check before moving on.

**Concept (fixed for all phases):** Best.Life is a free, private, no-signup *life-optimisation toolkit*. Flagship: a 4-minute "Life Score" across eight areas (Health, Mind, Love, Work, Money, Growth, Play, Purpose) with a radar chart and a 30-day plan. Around it: nine browser-only tools, eight pillar guides, practical articles, a curated + auto-updating video feed, a daily research-headline feed, 30-day challenges with a contest, a lead-gen "Blueprint" funnel, and donation/sponsor pledges. Revenue: Google AdSense first, then donations, sponsorship and partnerships.

---

## Phase 1 — Research and positioning
> Open and read at least 38 leading sites in the wellbeing / self-improvement / life-assessment / sleep / habit niches. For each record: what it does, interactive features, lead-gen method, monetisation, one design detail worth using. Summarise the pattern and the gap. Check existing trademarks containing "best life" and list the wording to avoid. Output `docs/RESEARCH.md`.

Check: ≥38 sites actually read; gap statement written; trademark notes written.

## Phase 2 — Architecture and design system
> Design a static site that runs on GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root with no build step on the server: plain HTML generated by one stdlib Python script (`build.py`), one CSS file, vanilla JS, relative links so it works at both a project sub-path and a custom domain. Define tokens (cream/ink/forest green/sunrise coral/gold), a serif display + sans body pairing, light and dark themes, sticky header, mobile menu, cards, forms, article layout with sticky aside. Meet WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion.

Check: every template renders at 360 px and 1280 px; dark mode legible.

## Phase 3 — Global chrome and compliance
> On every page: (1) a top strip reading exactly "Contact, if you are interested in this website/domain name/Sponsorship/Advertisement/Partnership", the whole line linked to the owner's enquiry page with the destination not shown in visible text; (2) the AdSense loader for publisher `ca-pub-6620975821265271` in `<head>` plus the `google-adsense-account` meta; (3) footer with trademark notice, legal links and "not professional advice" line; (4) cookie notice. Add `ads.txt`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, RSS, manifest, 404, `.nojekyll`.

Check: grep confirms the loader and top strip on 100% of pages.

## Phase 4 — Flagship: Life Score
> Build a 24-statement assessment (3 per area, 1–5 scale) with progress bar, validation that scrolls to the first unanswered item, 0–100 per-area scoring, overall score, a named "life shape", canvas radar chart, bars highlighting the two lowest areas, a 30-day plan for those two areas with links to the matching guide, history saved locally, share link carrying only the eight numbers, print/PDF, retake. After results, show an "email me my result" form.

Check: complete it end to end; scores and plan match manual calculation.

## Phase 5 — Tool suite
> Build nine local-first tools, each on its own page with 250+ words of original explanation and an FAQ (FAQPage schema): Sleep Calculator (both directions, user-set latency), Habit Tracker (7-day grid, streaks, 30-day %, export), Life in Weeks (grid canvas, stats, PNG download), Compound Growth (chart, table, inflation-adjusted), Daily Energy & Water (Mifflin–St Jeor, adults only, maintenance only), Breathing Timer (three patterns), Focus Timer (sessions counted), Gratitude Journal (daily prompt, export), Goal Builder (statement, checkpoints, daily minimum, if-then).

Check: each tool works with keyboard only and after a page reload.

## Phase 6 — Content for AdSense approval
> Write eight pillar guides (450–600 words) and twelve practical articles (300–400 words): original, useful, plainly written, consistent with mainstream public-health and financial-literacy guidance, with review dates and Article schema. Add About, Editorial Policy, Privacy & Cookies (including the Google advertising-cookie disclosures and opt-out links), Terms, Disclaimer, Contact. No thin pages, no placeholder text, no copied text, no fake testimonials or statistics.

Check: every indexable page has unique title, description and substantial body copy.

## Phase 7 — Lead generation
> Create a dedicated `blueprint.html` funnel: benefit-led headline, three proof points, a short form (name, email, focus area, optional challenge, consent), and a real deliverable — a printable 30-day plan that unlocks on the page immediately after signup. Repeat a compact version of the form as a band on the home page, every tool and every guide. Add an email-my-result form on the Life Score.

Check: submit a test lead; plan unlocks; confirmation shows.

## Phase 8 — Donations, sponsorship, hiring, contests
> `support.html`: four tiers, "where the money goes" (operations, promotion & marketing, hiring talent, contests & prizes), and a pledge form (no card data on site; payment link sent personally). `careers.html`: five contributor roles + application form. `challenges.html`: six 30-day challenges, a monthly "Finisher's Draw" with compliant rules (no purchase necessary, 18+, void where prohibited, not affiliated with Google/YouTube) and a registration form. Route business enquiries to the owner's enquiry link.

Check: all four forms deliver.

## Phase 9 — Forms and privacy of the inbox
> All forms post by AJAX to a form relay that forwards to the owner's single inbox. The address must never appear in page text, markup, repository files or documentation: store it only as an encoded array decoded at runtime. Include a honeypot, consent checkbox, success and failure states, and a fallback link to the enquiry page.

Check: repository-wide search for the address and its parts returns nothing.

## Phase 10 — Video and auto-updating data
> `videos.html`: editor's picks with topic filter plus "latest" feed; click-to-load embeds on the privacy-enhanced YouTube domain; cards whose thumbnail fails are removed. `research.html`: headline feed with source attribution. A stdlib script (`scripts/refresh.py`) verifies picks via oEmbed, pulls channel RSS and publisher RSS/Atom, and writes `data/videos.json` and `data/news.json`. A scheduled GitHub Actions workflow runs it daily and commits changes. Client-side: daily quote and two-minute challenge rotate by date, with a streak.

Check: workflow run is green and the JSON files change.

## Phase 11 — Trademark and copyright safety
> Brand the site only as the domain "Best.Life". Do not use the run-together or two-word forms as a brand, and do not use well-known slogans containing them. Publish a full trademark and copyright disclosure page and a short notice in every footer. Quotes must be public domain; headlines link out; videos are embedded, never copied.

Check: text search for risky phrasings is clean outside the disclosure itself.

## Phase 12 — Ship
> Commit to `main` of `webworksa1/best-life`, enable GitHub Pages from the branch root, confirm the live URL returns 200 for home, a tool, a guide, `ads.txt` and `sitemap.xml`, and trigger the refresh workflow once. Document the owner's remaining steps in `README.md`.
