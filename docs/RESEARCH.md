## What the research says

1. **The winning funnel is a free, no-signup assessment** with an instant visual result, then an *optional* email step (8, 15, 17, 21). → Life Score is the flagship and the primary lead source.
2. **Tools out-earn articles per visit and bring people back** (3, 24, 33, 40). Most competitors ship one tool to sell an app; nobody in the sample offers the full set free under one roof. → That gap is the positioning.
3. **"Your data never leaves your browser" is a conversion asset** (19, 20, 33, 34). → All tools are local-first.
4. **Ad + donation + sponsorship can coexist** (10, 24, 31, 40). → AdSense for baseline revenue, pledges and sponsored rounds on top.
5. **The existing "Best Life" publication is a listicle/deals magazine** (1). → A utility brand avoids both confusion and head-on competition.
