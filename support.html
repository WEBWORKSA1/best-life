Where your support goes

  • Ongoing operations — hosting, domains, maintenance and accessibility work.
  • Promotion & marketing — reaching people who would benefit from free tools.
  • Hiring talent — paying writers, subject reviewers, designers and developers.
  • Contests & prizes — funding rewards for people who finish a 30-day challenge.

How pledging works

  1. Send a pledge with the form. No card details are collected on this website.
  2. We reply personally with a secure payment link from an established payment provider.
  3. You receive a receipt and, if you wish, a place on the supporters list.

Donations are voluntary contributions to an independent project. They are not purchases, do not buy editorial influence, and unless we tell you otherwise in writing are not tax-deductible.
