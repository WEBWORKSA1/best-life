Money

How to build an emergency fund from zero

A step-by-step plan for your first financial buffer, even on a tight budget.

By the Best.Life editorial team · 2 min read · Reviewed 2026-11-19

An emergency fund turns a crisis into an inconvenience. It is the foundation every other money goal stands on.

Set the first target low

Forget six months of expenses for now. Aim for a starter buffer equal to one typical unexpected bill — a repair, a medical cost, an urgent trip. Hitting a small target builds belief.

Find the money

  1. Review subscriptions and cancel what you would not buy again today.
  2. Pick one spending category to trim by a third for ninety days.
  3. Send any windfall — refund, bonus, gift — straight to the fund.
  4. Sell three things you no longer use.

Keep it separate and boring

Use an easy-access savings account at a different bank from your everyday one. Out of sight helps. Name the account "Emergency" so future-you thinks twice.

Automate, then grow

Set a small automatic transfer for payday. When the starter target is reached, keep going toward one month of essentials, then three. If you have to use it, that is what it is for — refill it without guilt.

General education only, not financial advice.
