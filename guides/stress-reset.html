Mind

The five-minute stress reset you can do anywhere

A simple sequence — breathe, name, move, choose — for the moment stress spikes.

By the Best.Life editorial team · 2 min read · Reviewed 2026-11-19

Stress is useful in short bursts and corrosive when it never switches off. This sequence takes five minutes and needs nothing but a little privacy.

1. Breathe (2 minutes)

Inhale through the nose for four counts, exhale slowly for six to eight. A longer out-breath signals safety to the body. Use the Breathing Timer if you want a guide.

2. Name it (1 minute)

Say, silently or on paper, exactly what you feel: "I am anxious about the deadline," not "everything is a mess." Putting a precise label on an emotion tends to reduce its intensity.

3. Move (1 minute)

Stand, roll your shoulders, shake out your hands, walk to a window. Stress prepares the body to act; a little movement lets some of that energy go.

4. Choose one next step (1 minute)

Ask: what is the smallest useful action I can take in the next ten minutes? Then do only that. Overwhelm shrinks when it meets a specific task.

If stress is constant or you feel unable to cope, please talk to a health professional.
