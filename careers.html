Help build the toolkit

Best.Life is a small independent project that works with freelance and part-time contributors around the world. Roles open as supporter funding allows.

✍️

Contributing writer

Write clear, practical, well-sourced guides in one of the eight life areas.

🔬

Subject reviewer

Qualified health, psychology or finance professionals who check guides for accuracy.

💻

Front-end developer

Build fast, accessible, privacy-first tools in plain JavaScript.

🎨

Designer / illustrator

Shape the visual language of tools, printables and social content.

📣

Community & growth lead

Run challenges, contests and partnerships that bring the tools to more people.

How we work

  • Remote, asynchronous and outcome-based.
  • Paid per project or on a part-time retainer; terms are agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Evidence first: claims are sourced, and health and money content is reviewed.
  • Readers before revenue: no dark patterns, no fake urgency.
