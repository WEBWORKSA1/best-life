🔥 Daily Energy & Water Estimator

Estimate your resting and maintenance energy needs and a daily water guide, using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation.

What the numbers mean

Resting energy is an estimate of what your body uses in a day at complete rest — breathing, circulation, keeping warm. Maintenance multiplies that by an activity factor to approximate a normal day. The water guide is a simple body-weight rule of thumb; needs rise in heat and with exercise, and food and other drinks count too.

How to use it

Treat the output as a ballpark for understanding your body, not a target to hit. Appetite, sleep, stress and muscle mass all shift real needs from day to day. If you want to change your weight or have a health condition, a doctor or registered dietitian can give guidance fitted to you.

Designed for adults. Not suitable during pregnancy or for anyone with, or recovering from, an eating disorder — please seek professional support instead.

Frequently asked questions

Which formula is used?

The Mifflin–St Jeor equation, a widely used method for estimating resting energy expenditure in adults.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate. Individual results commonly differ by ten percent or more from measured values.
