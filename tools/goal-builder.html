🎯 Goal Builder

Turn a vague wish into a clear goal with a deadline, measure, checkpoints, a daily minimum and an if-then plan.

What makes a goal work

Goals fail for predictable reasons: they are vague, they have no deadline, nobody checks progress, and the first bad day ends them. The builder answers each of those. It writes a single clear statement, sets four checkpoints, defines a daily minimum for difficult days and prepares an if-then plan for your most likely obstacle.

After you build it

  • Print the plan or copy the statement somewhere you will see it daily.
  • Put the four checkpoint dates in your calendar now.
  • Tell one person. A little accountability goes a long way.
  • Track the daily minimum in the Habit Tracker.

Frequently asked questions

How many goals should I have at once?

One to three. Each additional goal divides your attention.

What if I miss a checkpoint?

Adjust the plan, not your self-worth. Shorten the scope or extend the date, and keep the daily minimum going.
