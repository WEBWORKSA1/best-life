📈 Compound Growth Calculator

See how regular saving grows over time, with a chart, yearly table and an inflation-adjusted figure.

Gold: what you put in. Green: total balance.

What compounding means

Compounding is growth earning its own growth. In the early years almost all of your balance is money you put in; later, the growth itself becomes the larger part. That is why the curve looks flat for a long time and then bends upward.

How this calculator works

It applies the yearly rate monthly, adds your contribution each month, and reports the final balance, the total you contributed and the growth earned. The "today's money" figure discounts the result by your inflation assumption so you can judge real buying power.

Using it wisely

  • Try changing the number of years before you change the amount. Time is the strongest input.
  • Returns are never smooth or guaranteed; use a conservative rate.
  • Fees and taxes, which vary by country and product, are not included.

For education only. This is not financial advice and not a forecast.

Frequently asked questions

What growth rate should I use?

That depends on what you hold and nobody can know future returns. Many people test a cautious, a middle and an optimistic rate to see a range.

Does it include fees and tax?

No. Subtract your expected annual fees from the growth rate for a closer estimate.
