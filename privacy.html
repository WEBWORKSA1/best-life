Privacy & cookie policy

What information Best.Life collects, how cookies and advertising work, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The tools on this site run in your browser. What you enter into them is not sent to us.
  • We receive personal details only when you choose to submit a form.
  • We show ads through Google AdSense, which uses cookies.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter signup, contact, pledge, contest registration, job application or emailed Life Score), we receive the details you enter — typically your name, email address and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox by a third-party form-relay service (FormSubmit), which processes the submission on our behalf. We use this information only to respond, to send what you asked for, to run the contest you entered, or to process your pledge. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

Tool data — Life Score history, habits, journal entries, timer counts, theme and cookie choice — is kept in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and you can erase it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. More about how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), consent for advertising cookies is requested through a consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Embedded content

Videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain only after you press play; YouTube may then set cookies under its own policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by a static hosting provider that may log technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and operations.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use the contact page. To stop emails, reply “stop” to any message.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date below.
