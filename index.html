How it works

Measure. Focus. Repeat.

Most self-improvement fails because it tries to fix everything at once. This does the opposite.

①

Measure

Answer 24 honest statements. Your Life Score shows eight areas side by side so the real gap is obvious.

②

Focus

Work only on your two lowest areas for 30 days, using one guide and one tool for each.

③

Repeat

Retake the score monthly. Your history is saved on your device so you can watch the shape change.
