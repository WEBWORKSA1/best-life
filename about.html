About Best.Life

Who we are, what we publish and how the project is funded.

Best.Life is an independent website that publishes free self-reflection tools and plain-language guides to living well. It exists for one reason: most advice about improving your life is either locked inside an app subscription or scattered across thousands of articles that contradict each other. We wanted one calm place that helps a person see where they stand and take the next small step.

What you will find here

  • The Life Score — a four-minute self-assessment across eight life areas.
  • Tools — private, no-signup utilities for sleep, habits, focus, money, calm and goals.
  • Guides — practical reading for each life area.
  • Videos and a research feed — curated and refreshed automatically.
  • Challenges — 30-day programmes and a community contest.

Our principles

  • Private by design. Tools run in your browser. We do not collect what you type into them.
  • Evidence-informed. We favour well-established findings over trends and say so when evidence is thin.
  • Small steps. We recommend the least effort that produces real change.
  • Honest funding. The site is funded by advertising, voluntary reader support and clearly labelled sponsorship.

Who is behind it

The site is operated by a small independent publishing team working with freelance contributors. It is not part of, or affiliated with, any magazine, media company, supplement brand or coaching programme. To reach us, use the contact page.
