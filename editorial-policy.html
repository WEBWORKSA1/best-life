Editorial policy

How Best.Life content is written, reviewed, corrected and funded.

How we write

Guides are written in plain language by the editorial team and contributors. We rely on consensus guidance from recognised public-health, medical and research bodies, on peer-reviewed research, and on long-standing professional practice. We avoid sensational claims and do not promise outcomes.

Review and updates

Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed. Health and money content is checked against current mainstream guidance, and pages are revised when that guidance changes.

Corrections

If you believe something is wrong or out of date, tell us through the contact page. We correct confirmed errors promptly.

Advertising and independence

The site displays advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense. Advertisers do not choose, review or influence editorial content. Sponsored content, when it exists, is labelled as such. We do not publish paid reviews or testimonials.

Third-party material

Videos are embedded with YouTube's standard player from their owners' channels. The research feed shows headlines with attribution and links to the publisher; we do not copy article text. Quotations are from public-domain sources or are brief and attributed.

Not professional advice

Everything here is general education. See the disclaimer.
